I have been a long time watcher of VlogBrothers but never actually read any of their books. Since I was looking for a new book to read I figured I'd check out John Green's latest. I picked it up from a local bookstore yesterday afternoon and finished it in an evening.

It was a fun read and I enjoyed it quite a bit but maybe not as much as I expected to. Perhaps its a bit too caught up in a "then this thing happened" kind of storytelling that compromises the book working as a psychological dive into the characters and vice versa. I think this is just my personal preference though. 

There were certainly parts of it I recognized from other John Green interviews and videos I've seen. Things like not having to share everything with the world.
