I bought this book about a year ago but I didn't finish it until this week. It is quite dense in many places asking you to think through example problems. Not quite a textbook but approaching one.

I do think this book gave me a powerful tool to think about the world and I would recommend it to anyone. The surprising results we can find when we cut out the noise in games and just get to different parties and their desired results can help explain how our democracy and systems for decision making can lead to worse outcomes. 

I especially liked the part about voting.

> When faced with a game\
> Look ahead and reason back\
> That's a game theory
