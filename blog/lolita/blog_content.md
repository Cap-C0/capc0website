Having finished this book feels like finally being in on a joke that only some people get. I suppose I was the sucker who went in to it expecting something more pornographic but I came out with the queer sensation of pleasant disappointment.

Humbert Humbert's desire for Lolita is a very strange one indeed. How can one feel such intense romantic passion for what one essentially views as an object? This might be reconciled if the H.H. came off as plainly narcissistic but he is a horrifyingly lucid observer of himself and a plainly terrible one of his beloved.

I liked when he shouted out Mission Dolores and Russian Gulch.
